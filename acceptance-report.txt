LANDING PAGE (Phase 5.4 — built this session, not just reconciled)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 5.4 was originally scoped as "confirm the landing page still matches
reference_design.pdf" — that assumption was wrong. No landing page existed
anywhere in the codebase; "/" simply redirected into the app across all of
Phases 0-3, despite DESIGN_SYSTEM.md carrying a full worked-out 9-section
composition (section 10) derived from reference_design.pdf since Phase 0.

Built web/src/pages/LandingPage.tsx following that composition exactly (hero,
product-proof panel with real live data from GET /api/events and
GET /api/gallery, a numbered rules list, an illustrative decision trail, four
capability cards, an illustrative dashboard preview, a full-bleed step band,
and a footer), plus the MetricTile primitive DESIGN_SYSTEM.md section 11
called for and never had. Routed at "/".

A real defect was found and fixed while building it: several `sm:` grid
classes fired their multi-column layout AT the 375px mobile breakpoint
(because this project's tokens redefine `sm` to mean 375px, not Tailwind's
default 640px) — the exact mistake the Phase 0/1 audit already documented and
named. Caught visually with a live 375px screenshot (a 5-column step band
crammed into one row), fixed by switching every such class to `md:`, and
re-verified at 375/768/1280 with zero horizontal overflow. "/" was then added
to breakpoints.spec.ts's PUBLIC_PAGES list so this is now a permanent,
automated regression check rather than a one-time manual look.
